How to play

Guess the country from its silhouette in 6 tries.

After each guess you see:

  • Distance to the target
  • Direction arrow (8-way)
  • Proximity % and colour bar

🟩 = close · 🟨 = medium · ⬛ = far

Daily puzzle resets at local midnight. Practice mode is unlimited.
